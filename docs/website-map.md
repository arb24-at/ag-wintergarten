# Wintergarten Website Map

This is a first website map based on `keywords v3.json`. It is a planning document only and does not contain full page content.

The map assumes the client may either provide full winter garden design/build services or repair-only services. Pages that may be irrelevant for a repair-only client are marked in the notes.

## Priority Logic

- **P1**: Strong commercial or urgent problem intent, likely to produce enquiries.
- **P2**: Useful supporting pages with decent demand or strategic internal linking value.
- **P3**: Informational, DIY, comparison, or lower-fit pages that should wait until the service scope is confirmed.

## Near-Duplicate Control

- Keep `wintergarten kaufen`, `wintergarten bauen lassen`, `wintergarten firma`, `wintergartenbau`, and `wintergarten nach mass` on one core build page.
- Keep `wintergarten kosten`, `wintergarten preise`, `kosten pro qm`, and `preis pro m2` on one pricing page.
- Keep `wintergarten undicht`, `wintergarten dach undicht`, and `wintergarten abdichten` on one problem/service page.
- Keep gasket and sealing terms on one `dichtungen erneuern` page unless the business has a separate window-repair service.
- Keep `wintergarten überdachung`, `terrassenüberdachung wintergarten`, and related roof/terrace cover terms on one page.
- If the client only provides repairs, avoid creating full build, type, material, DIY, and permit pages until their role is clearly defined.
